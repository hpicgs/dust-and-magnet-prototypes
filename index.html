The research model

From feature vectors
to explicit hypotheses.

High-dimensional datasets describe objects through many features, making relationships, similarities, and outliers difficult to interpret. Dust & Magnet visualization represents these objects as particles, with magnets attracting or repelling them according to feature values.

Analytical magnets extend this metaphor with criteria that can be combined interactively, including logical AND and OR, similarity, variance, and selected groups of features. Moving magnets and adjusting their influence lets analysts explore hypotheses while keeping the underlying dimensions and criteria inspectable. Particle placement combines analytical barycentric targets with collision-aware local relaxation to reduce overlap, providing an interpretable, human-in-the-loop approach to exploring feature spaces.
